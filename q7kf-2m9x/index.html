How the numbers are counted

  • Sale: an S+S Leads card marked won in Closed, EV/HP Closed or Commercial/ Strata Closed, dated the day it was moved there (or the day it was marked won, if that came first).
  • Revenue and values: the value on each card in GHL, exactly as entered. Open leads carry an estimate until quoted; closed sales should carry the real price (see Data health).
  • Win rate: sales closed in the period divided by sales closed plus leads lost or abandoned in the period.
  • New leads: cards created in the S+S Leads pipeline in the period.
  • Reviews: SolarQuotes only, from the SolarQuotes pull, as at 12 Nov 20:45. Google reviews are not included.
  • Lead cost: from the SolarQuotes and Solar Choice exports Jeremy downloads (scripts/lead-spend-import.ps1), ex GST. Cost per sale divides the spend in a period by the sales closed in it from that source, so it moves with timing; over a quarter or the FY it settles.
  • Rep: the owner of the Leads card in GHL at the time of the refresh. A deal handed to someone else as it closes counts for them; Data health shows sales owned by anyone other than Luke, Jack or Danny.
  • Test cards: any card with the word “test” in its name, its contact's name or a contact tag is left out of everything.
  • Periods: Canberra time. Weeks run Monday to Sunday; the financial year from 1 July.
